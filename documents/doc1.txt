Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems.
AI enables machines to learn from experience, adjust to new inputs, and perform human-like tasks.
Key applications of AI include expert systems, natural language processing, speech recognition, and machine vision.

AI is broadly categorized into two types: Narrow AI and General AI.
Narrow AI is designed to perform a specific task, such as facial recognition or internet searches.
General AI, also called Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), refers to machines that can perform any intellectual task a human can.

Machine learning is a subset of AI that gives systems the ability to automatically learn and improve from experience.
Deep learning is a further subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with many layers.
AI is transforming industries such as healthcare, finance, transportation, and education.

The history of AI dates back to the 1950s when Alan Turing proposed the Turing Test as a measure of machine intelligence.
Today, AI is used in self-driving cars, recommendation systems, chatbots, and robotic automation.
Ethical concerns around AI include bias in algorithms, job displacement, and the challenge of ensuring safe and transparent systems.
